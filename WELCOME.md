# Welcome to the Onto "Technical" Interview

Well, my version of it, anyway.

- We have 90 minutes. I cannot meaningfully determine your technical expertise in that timeframe in a way that does not include heavy bias for certain types of developers.
- I will not ask you to do any "complicated" algorithms or anything like that.
- There are no "time limits" for solving anything.

Instead....

- We'll take over where the last interviewer left off in implementing a sort of Wordle game clone.
- You may see some silly code. This is very realistic as you will see silly code in our company. We are a company of (mostly) humans, and we are all learning. I dare say we all look at the code we wrote three years ago and laugh.
- The goal is to make progress on this app and demonstrate how you work and think in a collaborative environment.
- In general, I don't expect you to have memorized algorithms or APIs, but I do hope that you will recognize "code smells" on the fly and tell me about them.

In doing so I hope we can both get a feel for whether or not we could work together on bigger projects in the future.

## Agenda

- Introductions and Overview
- Questions and Followup
- "Coding"
